About FRK

Challenge Conventional Fashion

FRK's mission is to challenge conventional fashion through experimental design, storytelling, and self-expression. Through distinctive clothing and immersive FRK-hosted events, we aim to build a creative ecosystem that connects people through fashion, art, music, sports, and culture.

Our Vision

FRK aims to become an internationally recognized fashion and lifestyle brand that extends beyond clothing. We plan to expand our collections, collaborate with emerging talent, and produce large-scale FRK events in cities around the world.

Founder & Creative Director: Michelle Vaquilar

A Creative Ecosystem

FRK caters to adults ages 21 and older who embrace individuality and express their personalities through personal style. Our audience includes artists, designers, musicians, athletes, and alternative fashion enthusiasts.

We reach customers through our online store, pop-up markets, and FRK-hosted events, primarily serving the San Francisco Bay Area and the broader U.S. market.
